# Account Recovery and Management

## Password Resets
If you forget your password, you can request a reset link from the login page. The link expires after 15 minutes. If you do not receive the email, please check your spam folder or ensure your company firewall is not blocking our domain.

## Account Lockouts
Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. To unlock your account, you must wait 30 minutes or have an administrator unlock it from the dashboard.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA)
2FA can be enabled via SMS or an Authenticator App. If you lose access to your 2FA device, you must use one of your recovery codes generated at setup. Support cannot disable 2FA for you without verifying your identity via a video call.

## Account Deletion (GDPR)
Users can request full account deletion under GDPR via the privacy settings page. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Note that any financial records must be retained for 7 years for tax compliance, even if the account is deleted.
