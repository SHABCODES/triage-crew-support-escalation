# Billing Policy and Refunds

## Standard Subscriptions
All standard subscriptions are billed monthly or annually on the date of signup. If a payment fails, the account will be given a 7-day grace period before being suspended.

## Refunds
- **Monthly plans:** We do not offer prorated refunds for monthly plans if you cancel mid-month.
- **Annual plans:** If you cancel an annual plan within the first 30 days, you are eligible for a full refund. After 30 days, no refunds are provided.
- **Double charges:** If you believe you were charged twice for the same billing period, please contact support immediately. We will verify and refund any duplicate charges within 3-5 business days.
- **Disputed charges:** Before initiating a chargeback with your bank, please reach out to us to resolve the issue. If a formal chargeback is filed, the account will be immediately locked pending investigation.

## VAT and Tax
For enterprise customers in the EU, VAT invoices are generated automatically. If your VAT number is missing from an invoice, you can update it in your billing settings and regenerate the invoice for the current year.
