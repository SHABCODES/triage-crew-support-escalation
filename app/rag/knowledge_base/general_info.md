# General Information and Sales

## Support Hours
Our standard support hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST. Enterprise customers have access to 24/7 priority support.

## Non-Profit Discounts
We offer a 50% discount on all plans for registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations. To apply, please email sales with your tax-exempt documentation.

## Feature Requests
We love hearing from our users! While we can't build every request (such as dark mode, which is currently on our roadmap for Q4), we track all feedback.

## Compliance
Our platform is SOC2 Type II compliant and GDPR compliant. You can request a copy of our latest audit report by signing an NDA with our sales team.

## Logos and Branding
You are welcome to use our logo in blog posts or press releases. You can find our official brand guidelines and high-resolution SVGs at /branding.
